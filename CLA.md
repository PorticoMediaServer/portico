## How to sign

To agree to this version, post this exact sentence as a comment on your pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The project records your GitHub username, account ID, the pull request and the time you signed. You only sign once for each version of this agreement.

If your employer or another organisation owns your work, an authorised representative also needs to sign a company agreement, which the maintainer will send you. A personal comment can't grant rights you don't own.
