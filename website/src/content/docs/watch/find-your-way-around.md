Use **Home**, **Search**, **Saved** and your libraries to find media. **Settings** holds your preferences and any server controls your account can use.

## Home

**Home** brings together what's most useful right now, including titles you haven't finished and recent additions. The rows you see can differ from one server or profile to another, within the libraries your account can use.

## Libraries

Under **Libraries**, choose one of the media collections your account can use. Movies, TV shows, anime, music and audiobooks have views suited to that kind of media. When recording is available, open **Channels → Recordings** for DVR recordings.

## Search

**Search** looks across every library available to you. Start typing on the Search page, then use its type filters to narrow the results. See [Search and save media](/docs/watch/search-and-saved/).

## Saved

**Saved** holds **My List**, **Favorites**, **Playlists**, **Collections**, **Saved views** and **History**. Personal saved items belong to the active profile.

## Your profile menu

Use your profile menu to switch profiles or servers, open **Account**, get help or sign out. On a narrow screen, the menu also includes **Settings**.

Under **Settings → Account**, the pages are **Profile & account**, **Playback**, **Audio & subtitles**, **Quality & data**, **Music**, **Appearance**, **Language & region**, **Privacy** and **This device**. Available controls depend on your device and account. **Profile & account** holds account security and connected devices. Choose **Playback**, **Audio & subtitles** or **Quality & data** for playback preferences.

Server administration appears under **Settings → Server** for accounts with permission to use it.
