**Home** brings together the items most useful right now, including unfinished titles and recent additions. The rows can vary by server and account.

**Libraries** opens the media collections your account can use. Movies, television, music, audiobooks, and recordings each have views suited to that kind of media.

**Search** looks across the libraries available to you. Use the quick search at the top of Portico for a short result list or open the full Search page for broader results and filters.

**Saved** keeps personal watchlists, playlists, collections, favorites, and saved views together when those features are enabled.

Your profile menu contains personal settings, account security, connected devices, and sign-out controls. Server administration appears only for accounts with permission to use it.
