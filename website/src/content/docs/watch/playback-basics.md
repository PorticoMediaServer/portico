Open a movie, episode, track, audiobook, recording, or live channel and choose its primary play action. Portico asks the server for a playback decision suited to the device and selected settings.

Progress is stored under your account. Stop on one device and the same item can resume from that point on another connected Portico app.

## Player controls

Available controls depend on the media and device. They can include play and pause, previous and next, short skip controls, volume, audio tracks, subtitles or lyrics, playback quality, queue, fullscreen, and close.

Closing the player saves the most recent confirmed position. Very short playback sessions or items near the beginning or end may be handled differently so accidental starts do not crowd Continue Watching.
