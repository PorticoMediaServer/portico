Recording needs permission for your account or profile, a working channel source, matching guide listings and recording storage. Open **Channels → Recordings** to manage DVR recordings in **Upcoming**, **Recorded**, **Series rules**, **History** and **Storage**.

## Record a showing

1. Open the program in the Live TV guide.
2. Select **Record**, review **Start early** and **End late**, then confirm **Record**.
3. Check the entry under **Channels → Recordings → Upcoming**.

## Record a series

Select **Record series…** when guide data identifies the program as part of a series. Choose new episodes or all episodes, this channel or any channel, and how many episodes to keep. Review **Start early** and **End late** so the recording covers the program without creating unnecessary overlaps.

You can review series rules under **Channels → Recordings → Series rules**. Changing or disabling a rule affects its upcoming recordings; check the resulting schedule.

## Recording defaults

The owner configures **Settings → Server → Live TV & Channels → Recording**. Defaults include start and end padding, keeping all recordings or a set count or number of days, and the policy for a recording that needs a tuner someone is watching. Portico keeps recordings as the original captured stream.

## Check the result

Portico checks the schedule against source capacity and other recordings. A scheduled entry isn't a finished recording. Check its state when the program starts and use [Resolve recording conflicts](/docs/live-tv/recording-conflicts/) if it can't run.

Completed and playable recordings appear under **Channels → Recordings → Recorded** and in the recording library available to their owner. Watch progress works as it does for other media. A partially captured program can be playable while still marked incomplete.
