title: Record a programme or series
description: Schedule one programme or keep new episodes from a series.
category: live-tv
order: 4
audience: Viewers
updated: 2026-07-12
---

Open a programme from the guide and choose **Record** for that showing. To keep new episodes, create a series schedule and review its channel, episode, repeat, and timing options.

Portico adds the schedule to DVR and checks it against tuner availability and other recordings. A schedule is not the same as a completed recording; confirm its state when the programme begins.

Recordings appear in the libraries and DVR views permitted for your account. Progress and watched state behave like other playable media.

If a schedule cannot run, review [Resolve recording conflicts](/docs/live-tv/recording-conflicts/) before changing the source or deleting the series schedule.
